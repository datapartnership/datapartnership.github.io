Leonardo Canon Rubiano is an Urban Transport Specialist in the World Bank’s Transport and ICT Global Practice. He is currently focused on providing technical support and leading analytical and activities in urban mass transit operations, demand modelling and data analytics in the East Asia Pacific region (Indonesia, Vietnam and Myanmar, mainly), and has worked extensively in Bank projects in Mexico, Colombia, Peru and Brazil. Prior to (re) joining the Bank in 2017, he held positions at the Colombian public sector, including Chief BRT Operations Officer with TransMilenio, Bogota’s mass transit authority, and infrastructure advisor to the Colombia National Planning Department for the Bogota Metro Line One Project. Leonardo holds a MSC degree in Urban Planning and Development from University College London, and an undergraduate degree in Civil Engineering from the Universidad de los Andes in Bogotá, Colombia. Leonardo likes teaching transport courses and is an aspiring airplane sports pilot. He is also a scuba diver, bass guitar enthusiast and trains to participate in half marathons.
