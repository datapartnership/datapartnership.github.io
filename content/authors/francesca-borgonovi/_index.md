Francesca Borgonovi is the Head of the Skills Analysis team in the OECD Centre for Skills at the Organisation for Economic Cooperation and Development (OECD). She is an expert on skills measurement and skills policies in a cross-country perspective. Her most recent work considers the impact of AI introduction in education and training systems; barriers to women’s participation in ICT sectors; the demand for AI professionals; the skills and employment implications of the green, digital and demographic transition; the impact of investments in health on human capital development; and the impact of disparities in skills development opportunities on economic growth.
