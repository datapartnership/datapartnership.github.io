Cheryl Li is a Research Analyst at the International Monetary Fund (IMF). Her work focuses on macroeconomic surveillance and quantitative economic research, with particular interests in financial stability, macroprudential policy, and macro-financial modeling. Her research also spans applied econometrics, fiscal policy, and the economic implications of artificial intelligence.

Prior to joining the IMF, she worked as a Data Analyst at the American Enterprise Institute (AEI), conducting quantitative research on U.S. housing markets and mortgage-rate dynamics. She holds a master’s degree in international economics and finance and a bachelor's degree in Financial Engineering.
