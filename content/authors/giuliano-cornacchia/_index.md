Giuliano Cornacchia is a geospatial data scientist and researcher at the National Research Council of Italy (CNR) in Pisa. He holds a PhD in Computer Science and specializes in road network analysis, routing algorithms, traffic simulation, and sustainable urban mobility. His research focuses on leveraging geospatial data and computational methods to better understand mobility patterns and support more efficient and sustainable transportation systems.
